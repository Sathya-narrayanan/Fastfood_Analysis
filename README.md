# Fastfood_Analysis

## 🔹 Project Overview

Analyzed fast food nutritional dataset to understand restaurant performance and food quality trends

Built interactive dashboards to compare calories, fat, sodium, cholesterol, protein, carbohydrates, and fiber across restaurants

Focused on identifying high-risk food categories and nutritional imbalances

Delivered insights to support healthier menu strategies and customer awareness

## 🔹 Tools & Technologies Used

SQL → Data querying and nutritional analysis

Microsoft Excel → Data cleaning and preprocessing

Power BI → Interactive dashboard creation and visualization

## 🔹 Key Features

Restaurant-wise nutritional comparison dashboard

Top 10 highest calorie, sodium, and cholesterol item analysis

Calorie, carbohydrate, and fiber distribution analysis

Relationship analysis between calories, fat, and sodium

Nutritional comparison across protein, vitamins, calcium, and fiber

## 🔹 Data Analysis Performed

Analyzed calorie distribution across restaurants and food categories

Studied sodium and cholesterol concentration in high-risk menu items

Evaluated relationships between fat, sodium, and calorie levels

Compared nutritional balance across restaurants using protein, fiber, vitamins, and calcium metrics

Identified high-calorie and low-fiber food trends impacting health quality

## 🔹 Dashboard Insights

### Restaurant Analysis

Sonic and McDonald’s showed the highest average calorie levels

Subway and Chick-fil-A provided relatively healthier nutritional profiles

### Nutritional Trends

Strong positive relationship between fat and calorie values

High sodium levels consistently observed across restaurants

### Customer Health Perspective

Majority of items fall within medium to high calorie ranges

Low fiber levels indicate limited healthy menu options

### High-Risk Food Analysis

Top sodium items exceeded recommended intake levels significantly

Burger and fried chicken items contained highest cholesterol levels

### Nutritional Balance

Subway showed higher fiber and vitamin content compared to competitors

Most restaurants relied heavily on calorie-dense and processed ingredients

## 🔹 Key Business Insights

Over 70% of menu items fall under medium to high calorie categories

Fat is the primary contributor to high-calorie food items

High sodium content is common across multiple fast food chains

Fiber-rich and nutritionally balanced options are limited

Restaurants offering fresh and customizable meals perform better nutritionally

## 🔹 Business Recommendations

Introduce more low-calorie and high-fiber menu options

Reduce sodium levels in processed and fried food items

Promote balanced meals with higher protein and lower fat content

Increase transparency through nutritional labeling and customer awareness

Adopt healthier cooking methods such as grilling and baking instead of deep frying

Develop healthier alternatives to improve long-term customer retention

## 🔹 Project Outcome

Delivered insights into fast food nutritional quality and restaurant performance

Identified high-risk nutritional patterns affecting customer health

Enabled data-driven recommendations for healthier menu optimization

Built a scalable Power BI dashboard for nutritional monitoring and comparative analysis
